reconkit

Passive OSINT recon for any domain: subdomains from certificate transparency, DNS over HTTPS, and TLS certificate intel. No target contact required.

$ python3 reconkit.py example.com
[*] DNS records (via DNS-over-HTTPS)
    A      104.20.23.154     MX    0 .
[*] Certificate-transparency subdomains (crt.sh)
    119 unique name(s)
[*] TLS certificate
    issuer : Cloudflare TLS Issuing ECC CA 3

Truly passive

Subdomains come from public certificate-transparency logs. The target never sees a packet.

DNS without dig

Full record sets over Cloudflare's DNS-over-HTTPS JSON API. Pure standard library.

Active when you need it

Optional wordlist brute force is clearly labeled and separated for authorized engagements.

$ python3 reconkit.py example.com -o recon.json

Active mode is for domains you own or are authorized to test. MIT License.
